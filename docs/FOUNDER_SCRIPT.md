## 1. The problem, then City Money — live landing

When a community gives someone a benefit, spending it should feel straightforward.

But once those credits move, how do you keep the programme’s eligibility rules intact?

That’s where City Money started.

I built it as a community-voucher experience where the rules travel with the credits. A participant can pay an eligible merchant, and the asset itself checks whether the recipient qualifies.

## 2. Cleanverse is the core — short motion explanation

Cleanverse is at the heart of that. CVI gives us identity eligibility. The CVA makes that eligibility a condition of every transfer.

We use Monad to settle the payment and make the outcome inspectable.

Now, what happens if the recipient doesn’t meet the rule?

Here I can check our lower-level comparison identity against the CVA. The contract rejects it.
